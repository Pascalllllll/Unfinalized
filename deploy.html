Deploy the board

This puts contracts/UnfinalizedBoard.sol on Base Sepolia from your wallet. You only do this once per copy of the game; every player then reads and writes the same board.

  1. Have a little Base Sepolia ETH in your wallet. It's test money: Base Sepolia faucets hand it out free.
  2. Press deploy and approve the transaction. Your wallet will ask to switch networks first if it needs to.
  3. Paste the address you get into src/config.js.
