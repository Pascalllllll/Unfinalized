Every stone in this tower is a real Ethereum transaction, stacked in the order it landed on mainnet. A new block arrives about every 12 seconds. Climb to the newest one before twelve confirmations close over you.

What the stones are
ETH transferWider the more ETH moved
Contract callSmall and dark
Contract deploymentThrows you upward
Blob transactionSlides back and forth
BlockA landing with its number cut in. The newest one is gold, or purple at night.

Full blocks pack their stones close together. Quiet blocks leave long gaps. The black floor is the 12-confirmation line: it sits twelve blocks under the newest block and rises every time one lands.
